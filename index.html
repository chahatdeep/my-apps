INSTALLING

  1. Download on the phone (or TV) you'll use it on.
  2. Open the file. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install.
  3. That's it. New versions come from inside the app: it offers them when they're out.

IF ANDROID WARNS YOU

Google Play Protect warns about apps that don't come from the Play Store, so MoreTube may be called "unsafe" or "harmful". You can still install it:

  1. On the warning, tap More details (or the small ⌄ arrow).
  2. Tap Install anyway. If it asks to send the app for a scan, either choice is fine.
  3. No Install anyway, only OK? In the Play Store, tap your profile picture → Play Protect → the gear, and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect just while you install. Turn it back on afterwards.

WATCH TOGETHER

  1. Tap the people icon at the top right and start a room.
  2. Friends type the 4-digit code, and everyone sees the same moment, play, pause, skip, and chat.
  3. Tap Add friend next to someone, and you can invite each other any time.
